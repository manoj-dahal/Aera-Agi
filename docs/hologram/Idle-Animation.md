# Idle Animation

Version: 1.0.0

---

# Overview

Idle Animations keep the avatar alive even when no conversation is occurring.

They prevent the avatar from appearing static while maintaining a natural presence.

---

# Idle Behaviors

- Blink
- Breathing
- Small Head Movement
- Eye Movement
- Smile
- Shoulder Movement
- Weight Shift
- Hand Adjustment
